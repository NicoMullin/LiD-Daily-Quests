# Daily Quests

The online game ran 808 one-off event quests: the anniversaries, the download
milestones, Valentine's, NEO W.E. and M.I.L.K., and the World of Tanks, No More
Heroes, killer7 and DEATHVERSE collabs. They come back as daily quests: a few
of them, picked at random for you, open on the quest board each game day.
Their names start with **[DAILY]**, so they stand out from the weekly and
event quests.

- **Quests a day**: 5 to begin with, up to 30. A new set opens at the game's
  daily reset (10:00 UTC), and when you use the manager's **Start a new game
  day**. They stay the same all day, restarts included, and every player gets
  their own.
- **Decal quests a day**: 1 to begin with (0 for none, up to all of them). Each
  of the 80 decals you cannot get any other way offline - from Guardmycin and
  the Pro Tips to Phoenix and Scornful Susie, plus the collab decals - has a
  quest of its own that pays it on the first clear.
- **Death Metal quests pay**: the rest of the day's quests pay Death Metals,
  more for harder quests (1 to 5 up to 4 to 20), and so does a decal quest after
  its first clear. They need the **Death Metals** mod; without it each Death
  Metal is paid as 5,000 Kill Coins, as the offline game does. Or pick each
  quest's own rewards from the online game.

## The collab decals and the Crossover pack

33 of the decals are collab decals the game hides on Steam (World of Tanks,
the killer7 Smiths, Kat, Lily Bergamo and more): without the **LET IT DIE
Crossover Content** pack they have no icon and no description. Quests paying
those only come up while the Crossover pack is on; without it, the other 47
decals are on offer. Turn the pack on whenever you like and they join in. If
there are fewer decal quests to pick from than you asked for, Death Metal
quests make up the day.

## The goals

The online game's goals for these quests were never kept, only their names,
counts and rewards. Each quest is given a real goal from the game's own weekly
quests: the same kind of goal, sized to its count, so a "x40" quest is a common
enemy and a "x2" one a boss. A quest only shows once you have reached the floor
its target lives on.

## It needs its plugin

The quests are picked by **lid-dailyquests.dll**, which comes with this mod and
is switched on and off with it. Without it no daily quest shows. Finish a quest
on the day it is open: at the next reset the day's picks change.

Tick it and click **Save Mod List**. Unticking it takes the quests away again;
decals and Death Metals already earned stay yours.
